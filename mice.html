How to read the specs

We don’t score or rank mice, because the “best” mouse is the one that fits your hand, grip, games and budget. Every review shows each spec on a plain-English scale (for example Ultralight under 60 g, Light 60–79 g, Medium 80–109 g, Heavy 110 g+). Grip, hand-size and Minecraft CPS fit are editorial estimates.
