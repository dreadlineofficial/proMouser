A general AI blends whatever it read on random websites, forums and old reviews into a belief. ProMouser AI only answers from ProMouser’s own mouse data, which people have checked.

Ask a normal chatbot and it may tell you the G102 is a great drag clicker. We only call a mouse a double-clicker once a person has verified it, and when we don’t know something, it says so.
