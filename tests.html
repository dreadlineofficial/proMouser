Test hub

Every mouse test, one place

8 free tools to measure your clicking, aim and reactions, and to check your mouse for faults like double-clicking switches, a jumpy scroll wheel or a low polling rate.

Lite stats

CPS test

Clicks per second from 1 to 60 seconds, with a moving-target mode, personal bests and history.

Double click test

Check whether your mouse registers single and double clicks cleanly.

Reaction time

Wait for green, then click. Five rounds, averaged, with your best saved.

Aim trainer

Hit 20, 30 or 50 targets as fast as you can. Tracks time, accuracy and time per target.

Polling rate test

See how often your mouse reports its position: 125, 500, 1000 Hz or more.

Scroll wheel test

Catch a scroll wheel that jumps the wrong way, a classic sign of a worn encoder.

Mouse button test

Press every button, including side buttons, and spot switches that double-click by themselves.

Ultra

Sensitivity converter

Convert your sensitivity between CS2, Valorant, Apex, Overwatch 2 and more.

A 5-minute routine

Warm up like a pro

  1. Reaction test, 1 round of 5, to wake up.
  2. Aim trainer, 30 medium targets.
  3. CPS test, one 5 s run, then one 10 s run.
  4. Once a month: button, scroll and polling tests to catch a failing mouse early.
