Grip types

Three common grip styles explained

Palm grip

Best for relaxed control and larger mice. Great for longer sessions and comfort-first setups.

Claw grip

Offers fast movement and quick flicks. Often preferred by players who want more control.

Fingertip grip

Light touch and quick response. Works well with smaller, lighter mice and faster play.

How to pick

Try matching your grip to your hand size and preferred button reach before buying a new mouse.

Practical tips

What to watch for

Look for a mouse that feels natural in your grip, keeps your wrist aligned, and doesn't force you to overextend your fingers. If you game for long sessions, comfort matters as much as sensor performance.
