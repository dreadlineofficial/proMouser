CPS Feedback

Minecraft-related performance guide

Use this guide as a quick reference for what your CPS means in practice.

1–4 CPS

Very low click rate. Suitable for basic gameplay and casual survival, but not effective for competitive PvP.

5–6 CPS

Entry-level performance. Viable for casual PvP, but you will generally lose trades and combos against more experienced players.

7–8 CPS

Respectable click rate. Solid for standard PvP scenarios; with good aim and movement, this is more than sufficient for consistent performance.

9–10 CPS

Above-average performance. Players at this level can begin to take and maintain combos reliably, assuming decent mechanics and positioning.

11–12 CPS

Advanced click rate. Typically indicates refined clicking technique and allows strong pressure in close combat, provided control is maintained.

13–14 CPS

High-level PvP performance. At this rate, you can apply sustained offensive pressure and capitalize on openings, assuming your aim and game sense are developed.

15–16 CPS

Very high click rate. Common among serious PvP players using advanced techniques. Effective only if you can maintain accuracy and consistency.

17–18 CPS

Competitive-level speed. At this point, the limiting factors are usually aim, timing, and consistency rather than raw CPS alone.

19–20 CPS

Exceptional mechanical speed. If achieved legitimately with control and accuracy, this is highly impressive.

20+ CPS

Focus more on other aspects — your CPS is already sorted, and your aim, timing, positioning, and game sense will matter more than raw speed.
