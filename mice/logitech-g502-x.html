Logitech G502 X

Lots of buttons in a much lighter G502 body.

The Logitech G502 X is a medium-weight wired ergonomic mouse with the Logitech HERO 25K sensor. It weighs 89 g, has 13 buttons and suits palm and claw grips and large hands. It’s mainly for MMOs and MOBAs and work and creative apps.

Minecraft CPS

Can’t double-click

It can’t double-click. Its optical switches don’t bounce, so every press is exactly one click and butterfly or drag clicking can’t add extra ones.

Our take

Strengths and trade-offs

Worked out from the spec sheet and how the shape and switches behave in games. We haven’t written a hands-on review of this one yet.

Why you’d pick it

  • Flagship sensorThe Logitech HERO 25K has far more range than you need, so tracking is never the bottleneck.
  • Lots of buttons13 programmable buttons for binds, macros and shortcuts.
  • Very comfortableA contoured shape that’s easy to hold for hours.

Think twice if…

  • Can’t double-clickButterfly and drag clicks won’t add extra clicks, so for 1.8 PvP pick a mouse that double-clicks.
  • Right-handed onlyThe sculpted shape won’t work for left-handed players.
  • LargeSmaller hands may struggle to reach every button.
