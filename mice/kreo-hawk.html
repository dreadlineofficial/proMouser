Kreo Hawk

A comfy, budget ergonomic mouse with a proper sensor.

The Kreo Hawk is an Indian-brand wired mouse with a sculpted right-hand shape, a thumb rest, 8 programmable buttons and a PixArt 3327 sensor for around ₹1,199. It’s comfortable for long sessions and fine for FPS. ProMouser tested it for Minecraft clicking: it can’t double-click, so it isn’t a 1.8 PvP pick.

Why it stands out

Comfort on a budget

What you get for around ₹1,200, and what you don’t.

Why you’d pick it

  • Comfortable shapeA sculpted right-hand shell with a thumb rest, easy to hold for hours.
  • A real sensorThe PixArt 3327 tracks cleanly for everyday play and FPS.
  • 8 programmable buttonsSide buttons and software for binds and macros.

Think twice if…

  • Can’t double-clickButterfly and drag clicks won’t add extra clicks, so for 1.8 PvP pick a mouse that double-clicks.
  • Not lightAt about 110 g it’s steadier than it is quick to flick.
  • Right-handed onlyThe ergonomic shape won’t work for left-handed players.
ProMouser take

Who it fits best

Good fit if

You want a comfortable palm-grip mouse for work, casual FPS or 1.9+ Minecraft on a small budget.

Consider alternatives if

You play 1.8 PvP: it can’t double-click. The Ant Esports GM320 does for ₹498, and the Glorious Model D gives you a similar ergonomic shape that double-clicks.

Verdict

A solid, comfy budget mouse. Just don’t buy it for CPS.
