Redragon M711 Cobra

The classic cheap RGB gaming mouse.

The Redragon M711 Cobra is a medium-weight wired ergonomic mouse with the PixArt PMW3325 sensor. It weighs 100 g, has 7 buttons and suits palm and claw grips and medium and large hands. It’s mainly for Minecraft and work and creative apps.
