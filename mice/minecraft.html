Quick setup tips

Use the right mouse for the way you play

Palm grip

Best if you want relaxed control, especially for long mining or building sessions.

Claw / fingertip grip

Good if you prefer quick camera movement, fast flicks, and a slightly lighter feel.

Buttons & bindings

Use extra buttons for sprint, inventory shortcuts, or quick utility actions to reduce hand strain.
