Endgame Gear XM1r

Crisp, low-latency clicks for jitter and butterfly.

The Endgame Gear XM1r is a light wired symmetrical mouse with the PixArt PAW3370 sensor. It weighs 63 g, has 5 buttons and suits claw and fingertip grips and medium hands. It’s mainly for FPS and Minecraft.
