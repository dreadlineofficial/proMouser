Corsair Harpoon RGB Pro

A small, grippy budget mouse.

The Corsair Harpoon RGB Pro is a medium-weight wired ergonomic mouse with the PixArt PMW3327 sensor. It weighs 85 g, has 6 buttons and suits claw and palm grips and small and medium hands. It’s mainly for work and creative apps and FPS.

Minecraft CPS

Can’t double-click

It can’t double-click. Like most mice, its firmware ignores a second press that comes too soon after the first (debounce), so butterfly and drag clicks get merged into single clicks.
