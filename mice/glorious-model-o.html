Glorious Model O

A Minecraft favorite for butterfly clicking and fast control.

Almost every experienced Minecrafter has heard of the Glorious Model O. It’s famous for letting users lower debounce to 0ms, which can make one finger movement register as two clicks and help with faster clicking techniques.

Why it stands out

What makes the Glorious Model O worth considering

0ms debounce setting

Enables faster input behavior for users who want to push their clicking style and experiment with high-CPS techniques.

Lightweight and balanced

It weighs 67g and is balanced well enough that the added weight is often hard to feel during normal play.

Tracking and DPI

Tracking reaches 250+ IPS on wired and 400+ IPS on wireless, with up to 12,000 DPI on wired and 19,000 on wireless.

RGB and buttons

Customizable RGB and 6 fully programmable buttons make it flexible for both play and daily use.

ProMouser take

What we noticed while using it

Best for butterfly and jitter clicking

The buttons feel easy to control and are very comfortable for fast, repeated clicks. It is not the best choice for drag click.

Small limitations

The software is a little older and doesn’t offer as many lighting or customization options as newer mice, but it is still good enough for everyday use.

Our rating

For players who want a fast, light, and responsive mouse for Minecraft and clicking practice, this is a strong pick. Our overall takeaway is 9.5/10.
