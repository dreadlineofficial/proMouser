Ant Esports GM320

The cheapest mouse we’ve verified can double-click.

At around ₹498, the GM320 is the budget way into high-CPS Minecraft PvP: ProMouser has verified that it double-clicks, so butterfly and jitter clicking register extra clicks the way they do on a Glorious Model O. The trade-off is build: the shell feels less solid and the stock skates glide less smoothly than the pricier double-clickers.

Why it stands out

Double clicks for under ₹500

What you get, and what you give up compared with the pricier double-clickers.

Why you’d pick it

  • Verified double-clickerProMouser has confirmed it double-clicks, so butterfly and jitter clicks register extra clicks.
  • Absurd valueAround ₹498, roughly a twelfth of a Glorious Model O.
  • 8 programmable buttonsSide buttons and software for binds and macros.
  • RGB and a braided cableLooks the part, and the cable doesn’t snag as much as rubber ones.

Think twice if…

  • Build and skatesBuild quality and the stock skates are a step below the other double-clicking mice: it glides less smoothly and feels less solid.
  • Heavier than PvP favouritesIt’s a full-size wired mouse, so it isn’t as quick to flick as a 60 g ultralight.
  • Unpublished specsAnt Esports doesn’t publish the sensor, switches or weight.
ProMouser take

Who it fits best

Good fit if

You want to butterfly or jitter click in 1.8 PvP and your budget is tight. It does the one thing most mice can’t: double-click.

Consider alternatives if

You care about a premium feel, smooth glide or a light shell. The Glorious Model O, Model O- and HyperX Pulsefire Haste 2 double-click too and are better built.

Verdict

The best value double-clicker on ProMouser. Slightly behind the rest on build quality and skates, far ahead on price.
