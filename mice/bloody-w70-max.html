Bloody W70 Max

Bloody’s 10K-CPI RGB model.

The Bloody W70 Max is a medium-weight wired ergonomic mouse with the Bloody BC3332-A sensor. It weighs 108 g and suits palm and claw grips and medium and large hands. It’s mainly for Minecraft and FPS.

Minecraft CPS

Can’t double-click

It can’t double-click. Like most mice, its firmware ignores a second press that comes too soon after the first (debounce), so butterfly and drag clicks get merged into single clicks.
