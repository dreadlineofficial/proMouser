Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ProMouser is a static website for mouse guides and browser-based tools. This policy explains what the site itself stores and what happens when you use external services linked from the site.

Information stored in your browser

The site does not require an account. A few features store small preferences locally in your browser:

  • The announcement banner dismissal is remembered for the current browser session using session storage.
  • The mouse comparison tool saves its selected comparison settings in local storage so they remain available on your device.
  • ProMouser AI processes your questions in the page and reads the local mouse-guide pages to provide context. It does not send your chat text to an AI service.
  • In demo mode, account details (including your name, email, password hash, profile photo, username, bio, location, website, preferences, credits, plan, orders and recommendation history) are stored in this browser under pm_users and pm_session. They are not sent to ProMouser.
  • When Supabase sign-in is configured, account details and profile fields are stored in the ProMouser Supabase project. The site also keeps a local account cache and sign-in session in browser storage. If you connect Google or Discord, that provider handles its sign-in flow and shares the identity information needed to authenticate your account. Profile photos are stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket, so anyone with the photo URL can view the image; do not upload a private photo.
  • A plan marker (pm_plan) is kept in local storage so the ProMouser Basic, Lite or Ultra colour theme can apply as soon as a page loads.
  • ProMouser Ultra members can save a price watchlist, preference profiles and assistant memory with their account. The sensitivity converter remembers your last inputs in local storage (pm_sens).
  • The CPS test and the other mouse tests keep your scores, personal bests and test settings in local storage (pm_cps and pm_tests) so you can track progress. They stay in your browser; clear them with the Clear button on the CPS test or your browser’s site data settings.
  • Session storage (pm_onb_asked) remembers whether the welcome questions have already been shown during the current visit.
  • If you use ProMouser AI, your messages and the preferences it picked up are kept in session storage (pm_assistant) for the current tab only, so the conversation survives a reload. Closing the tab deletes them; they are never sent anywhere.

Browser values are not sent to ProMouser unless a feature explicitly uses the configured account service. You can clear local values through your browser’s site-data controls.

Accounts, credits and payments

Accounts can run in local demo mode or use Supabase for cross-device sign-in and profile storage. Checkout is currently a demonstration: no payment details are collected and no real payments are taken. This policy will be updated before real payments are introduced.

External services

Pages load typography from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to request those font files. Links to services such as YouTube, retailers, and Logitech open their own websites; those services have separate privacy practices that ProMouser does not control.

Analytics and advertising

ProMouser does not intentionally use its own analytics or advertising trackers.

Children and sensitive information

The site is not designed to collect personal or sensitive information. Please do not include private information in messages or reports submitted to third-party services.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the site’s features change. For questions, visit the contact page.
